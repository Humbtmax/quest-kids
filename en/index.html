Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know to get started today with peace of mind.

Does it work with any school? expand_more
Yes. You send the material your child is using, from any public or private school, and the lessons are built from it.
Does it work on computers, tablets and phones? expand_more
Yes. It runs in the browser, nothing to download, and progress is saved in the cloud: your child can start on the computer and continue on the iPad.
How much time per day? Can they study more? expand_more
You choose from 15 minutes to 1 hour a day and can change it anytime. We recommend 15 to 25 minutes. After today’s lesson, they can repeat it or practice what they missed as many times as they like.
What ages is it for? expand_more
For kids aged 8 to 11, 3rd to 6th grade.
Does the AI give my child the answers? expand_more
No. It asks questions, gives hints and explains after your child tries. The goal is learning, not copying.
What happens to the photos I send? expand_more
They’re stored privately, used only to create the lessons, and deleted as soon as the lessons are ready. We only ask for your child’s nickname — never a full name, school or face photo.
Are there ads? expand_more
No. No ads, no in-app purchases for kids and no links to the open internet.
How do I cancel? expand_more
You cancel yourself from the parent dashboard, anytime. There’s no charge during the free trial.
